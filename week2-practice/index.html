Stage 1: Build a short film programme by selecting films from the list below.

Add your first film to the programme by clicking on the "Add" button next to the film title.

Stage 2: Once you have added a few films to your programme, you can view your selections by clicking on the "View Programme" button below.

Stage 3: If you want to remove a film from your programme, simply click on the "Remove" button next to the film title in your programme list.

Click on the "Remove" button to delete a film from your programme.
